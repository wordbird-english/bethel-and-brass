ABOUT BETHEL & BRASS

Rooted in words.
Made for living.

Bethel & Brass is a two-owner studio building wall art in Hebrew and English. We draw on scripture, greetings and the language of home, giving each its own place and its own honest attribution.

Some works quote a verse. Others adapt a phrase or offer a greeting. Every piece is made to invite a closer look and a more thoughtful room.
