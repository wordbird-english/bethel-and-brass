CHOOSING YOUR PRINT

Hebrew and English wall art: choosing a verse or a greeting

Start with the words you want to see each day. A Bible passage, a greeting and an original blessing can all belong in a home, but they are different kinds of text. Check which one a print uses before you choose it.

Our Pray for the Peace of Jerusalem print is Psalm 122:6-inspired artwork. It pairs Hebrew above English with an illustration of Jerusalem's walls and terraces. The product page describes the artwork as inspired by the verse, rather than naming a complete translation edition.

Shalom in This Home takes a different approach. It pairs the Hebrew word Shalom with an original English blessing and an olive branch. Its words are an original sentiment, not a Bible quotation or a word-for-word translation of a Hebrew verse.

Look at the product artwork as well as any room view. The Shalom in This Home gallery labels its room view as a visualization, not a product photograph. Both Shalom in This Home and Pray for the Peace of Jerusalem are available as 12 x 16 inch paper art prints. Measure that size where you plan to display yours, and check the final product details before ordering.

Choose the words first, then the design that fits your space. Tax, shipping and delivery details are shown at checkout.
